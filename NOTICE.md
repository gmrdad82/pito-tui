# Notices

The look this shell draws with comes from the crates it brings, and is in the
style of HEY's terminal UI by 37signals
([basecamp/hey-cli](https://github.com/basecamp/hey-cli)): the centred rules
and nested numbered tabs of pito-header, the "key label · key label" hints and
"Yes ▸ No" confirm of pito-footer, the selected-row marker of pito-list, and
the hourglass of pito-hourglass, whose frames and timing come from hey-cli
under its MIT licence (see pito-hourglass's own NOTICE.md). No code or text of
hey-cli is copied into this crate.
